Melatonin is a hormone your body makes naturally as it gets dark. It tells your brain that it is time to sleep. A melatonin supplement can help you fall asleep, especially when your schedule, travel or screen time throws your rhythm off.*

## How to take melatonin gummies

- **Timing:** take them 30 to 60 minutes before your planned bedtime.
- **Serving:** start with the suggested serving on the label. More is not better.
- **Setting:** dim the lights and put your phone down after you take them. Bright screens work against melatonin.

## Why melatonin with passion flower?

Some sleep gummies pair melatonin with botanicals like **passion flower**, which has been used for generations to promote evening relaxation, and **vitamin B6**, which plays a role in producing neurotransmitters.*

## Bedtime habits that make a difference

A supplement works best alongside good sleep habits:

1. Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends.
2. Keep your bedroom cool, dark and quiet.
3. Avoid caffeine after early afternoon.
4. Stop using screens 30 to 60 minutes before bed.
5. Get daylight in the morning to anchor your body clock.

## Safety tips

Do not drive or operate machinery after taking melatonin. Talk with your healthcare provider before use if you are pregnant or nursing, take medications (including blood thinners, sedatives or blood pressure medications), or have a medical condition. Keep sleep gummies out of reach of children.
