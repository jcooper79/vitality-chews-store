Ashwagandha (*Withania somnifera*) is an herb that has been used in Ayurvedic tradition for thousands of years. Today it is one of the most popular supplements in the US, mostly because people want help handling everyday stress.*

## What is an adaptogen?

Ashwagandha is called an **adaptogen**, a term for herbs that help the body adapt to everyday physical and mental stressors. Research on ashwagandha root extract has focused on how it supports a healthy stress response and helps maintain cortisol levels already within the normal range.*

## Why choose a gummy?

Capsules and powders work, but they are easy to forget and some taste bitter. A gummy turns your supplement into something you look forward to, which makes it easier to stay consistent. Consistency matters with ashwagandha because most people take it daily for several weeks before deciding how it works for them.

## When should you take ashwagandha?

There is no single best time. Pick a time you will remember every day:

- **Morning**, with breakfast, as part of your start-the-day routine
- **Evening**, as part of winding down

The most important thing is to take it consistently and not exceed the suggested serving on the label.

## What to look for on the label

- **Made in the USA in a GMP-certified facility.** GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) standards cover how supplements are made, tested and labeled.
- **Third-party testing** for purity, including heavy metals.
- **A clear suggested serving** and a full ingredient list.

## Who should talk to a doctor first?

Talk with your healthcare provider before taking ashwagandha if you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription medications (especially for thyroid, blood pressure, blood sugar or mood), or have a medical condition.
