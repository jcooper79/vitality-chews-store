This Privacy Policy explains what information Vitality Chews ("we", "us") collects when you visit vitalitychews.com or place an order, and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Order information:** your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and shipping and billing addresses.
- **Payment information:** payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Basic site data:** standard information your browser sends, such as pages visited and device type, used to keep the site secure and working.

Your shopping cart is stored in your own browser until you check out.

## How we use your information

We use your information to process and ship your order, send order and shipping notifications, answer your questions, prevent fraud, and meet our legal obligations.

## Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to complete your order with:

- **Our payment processor (Stripe)** to take payment
- **Our fulfillment partner and shipping carriers** to pack and deliver your order

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data retention

We keep order records as long as needed to provide service, handle exchanges, and meet tax and accounting requirements.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing [hello@vitalitychews.com](mailto:hello@vitalitychews.com). We will respond within 30 days.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it was last updated.
