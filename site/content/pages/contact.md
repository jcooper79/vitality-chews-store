## Other ways to reach us

Use the form above or email **[hello@vitalitychews.com](mailto:hello@vitalitychews.com)**. We reply within one business day, Monday through Friday.

To help us find your order quickly, include your **order number** (it starts with VC-) and the email address you used at checkout.

## Common questions

- **Where is my order?** You will receive an email with tracking as soon as your order ships. Check your spam folder if you do not see it. See our [Shipping Policy](/shipping-policy/) for timelines.
- **Can I exchange a product?** Yes, within 30 days. See our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy/).
- **Product questions?** Visit our [FAQ](/faq/) or the product page for suggested use and ingredients.
