## Supplements you will actually take

Most supplement routines fall apart for one simple reason: the pills are big, the routine is a chore and the bottle ends up in the back of the cabinet. Vitality Chews started with a simple idea. If your daily supplements taste good, you will take them every day, and consistency is what counts.

So we built a focused line of chewable gummies for the things adults care about most: a calm mood, restful sleep, immune support, comfortable joints and complete daily nutrition.*

## How our gummies are made

- **Made in the USA** in a GMP-certified, FDA-registered facility
- **Third-party tested** for purity, including heavy metals and microbials
- **Made for adults**, with clear labels and suggested servings
- **Shipped fast**, usually leaving our fulfillment center within 1 to 2 business days

## Our promise

We keep the line small on purpose so every product earns its place. We describe what each formula is designed to support, and we do not make promises that a supplement cannot keep.
