## Free shipping on orders of $75 or more

Orders with a subtotal of $75 or more ship free within the United States. Orders under $75 ship for a flat rate shown at checkout.

## Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within the United States, including APO/FPO addresses.

## Processing time

Orders are packed at our US fulfillment center in Lawrenceville, Georgia, Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. Most orders leave the fulfillment center within **1 to 2 business days**. An order placed on Friday afternoon or over the weekend usually ships on Monday or Tuesday.

During high-volume periods, such as the holiday season, processing may take a little longer.

## Delivery time

We ship with USPS. After your order leaves our fulfillment center, delivery typically takes **3 to 7 business days**. Carrier delivery estimates are not guaranteed and can be affected by weather, holidays and carrier volume.

## Tracking your order

You will receive an email when we receive your order and another with your tracking number when it ships. Tracking updates come from the carrier and can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created. If you do not see our emails, check your spam folder.

## Lost or damaged packages

If your tracking has not updated for 7 business days, or your package arrives damaged, email us at [hello@vitalitychews.com](mailto:hello@vitalitychews.com) with your order number and we will make it right.

## Address changes

Email us as soon as possible if you need to change your shipping address. Once a label has been created, we may not be able to change it.
