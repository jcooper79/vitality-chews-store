Our policy is valid for a period of 30 calendar days from the date of purchase. We do not offer refunds, but you can return a product for an exchange. If more than 30 days have passed since your purchase, we are unable to offer an exchange.

## Exchange requirements

The following criteria must be met to qualify for an exchange:

- The product is defective
- The product is in its original packaging
- The product is unused

All returns are inspected to confirm these criteria are met. If a product does not meet them, we reserve the right not to issue an exchange. Perishable goods are exempt from return.

## Proof of purchase

To complete your exchange, we require a receipt, order confirmation or other proof of purchase.

## Sale and clearance items

Only regular-priced items may be exchanged. Sale and clearance items cannot be exchanged.

## Sending a return

Contact us at [hello@vitalitychews.com](mailto:hello@vitalitychews.com) before sending a return. Returns can be mailed to: 1615 Lakes Pkwy, Suite C, Lawrenceville, GA 30043.

You are responsible for the cost of shipping items you wish to exchange, and shipping costs are not refunded. Please package items carefully so they are not damaged in transit. If a product arrives damaged or used beyond what is needed to inspect it, we may reject the exchange.

## Contact us

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy, email us at [hello@vitalitychews.com](mailto:hello@vitalitychews.com).
