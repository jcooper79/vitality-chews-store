## Who are Vitality Chews made for?

Vitality Chews gummies are made for adults 18 and older. They are not intended for children.

## Do your gummies contain artificial colors or flavors?

Our gummies are made with natural flavors and colors. Always check the product label for the full ingredient list and allergen information.

## Can I take Vitality Chews with medications?

Talk with your healthcare provider before combining any supplement with medications, or if you are pregnant, nursing or managing a medical condition.

## How many gummies should I take?

Suggested use is listed on every product page and label. Most of our gummies are taken as two gummies once a day. Do not exceed the suggested serving.

## How long until I notice a difference?

Results vary from person to person. Most people use a supplement consistently for several weeks before deciding how it works for them.

## Where are your gummies made?

Every Vitality Chews product is made in the USA in a GMP-certified, FDA-registered facility and third-party tested for purity.

## How long does shipping take?

Orders usually leave our fulfillment center within 1 to 2 business days, and USPS delivery typically takes 3 to 7 business days after that. Orders of $75 or more ship free. See our [Shipping Policy](/shipping-policy/).

## What is your return policy?

You can exchange unused products in original packaging within 30 days of purchase. See our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy/) for details.

## How should I store my gummies?

Keep the bottle tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and out of reach of children.
